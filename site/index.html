Opportunity score, 0 to 100: half comes from how far the returning players at your position fall short of a typical roster share (pitchers 45%, infield 25%, outfield 17%, catchers 10%), about a third from the share of that position who are seniors or grad students, and the rest from how few freshmen and sophomores are behind them. It points you to programs worth a conversation; it is not a verdict on any coach's plans.

How the data stays current

  1. Rosters and coaches, dailyMost athletic sites run on the same two platforms (Sidearm and PrestoSports), so one parser reads the roster and coaches pages of hundreds of programs. It records position, class, hometown and the staff emails each site publishes.
  2. Camps, dailyPrograms post camps on their own news pages and on registration sites such as Ryzer. The agent searches both and keeps only events at that school, then marks each one upcoming or past.
  3. Tuition and enrollment, yearlyThese change once a year, so they come from each school's cost page and the federal College Scorecard and IPEDS data rather than a daily scrape.
  4. Change alertsWhen a page disagrees with itself, such as a coach listed on the roster page but not the staff page, or a roster that has not been posted for the new season, the profile flags it instead of guessing.
